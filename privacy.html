Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 24, 2026 | Last Updated: August 24, 2026

At PDF BOX (accessible from https://www.pdfbox.online), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by PDF BOX and how we use and protect it.

Privacy Pillar

Zero-Retention & Ephemeral Document Processing

Your uploaded documents are processed entirely by automated algorithms without human intervention. Uploaded PDF files and converted output documents are permanently deleted from our servers immediately after download or within 60 minutes of inactivity. We never store, inspect, sell, or train AI models on your files.

1. Information We Collect

PDF BOX operates on a privacy-first principle. You are not required to create an account, register, or provide personal details to use our core document tools.

  • Uploaded Document Data: Files uploaded for conversion or manipulation are held in temporary, sandboxed memory spaces strictly for the duration of the requested operation and are destroyed immediately afterwards.
  • Voluntary Contact Information: If you contact us directly via email (support@pdfbox.online) or our contact form, we may receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

2. Log Files & Analytics

PDF BOX follows standard industry procedures for using server log files. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any personally identifiable information. The purpose of this information is to analyze server health, prevent denial-of-service (DoS) attacks, administer the site, and optimize site performance.

3. Cookies and Advertising Partners

Like any other website, PDF BOX uses "cookies" to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. The information is used to optimize the user experience by customizing our web page content based on visitors' browser type and device preferences.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based on their visit to www.pdfbox.online and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info/choices.

4. Third-Party Advertising Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on PDF BOX. They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note: PDF BOX has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.

5. GDPR Data Protection Rights (European Union)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • The right to access: You have the right to request copies of your personal data.
  • The right to rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you believe is inaccurate.
  • The right to erasure: You have the right to request that we erase your personal data, under certain conditions. (Note: Uploaded files are automatically erased immediately upon conversion).
  • The right to restrict processing: You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal data.
  • The right to data portability: You have the right to request that we transfer the data that we have collected to another organization, or directly to you.

If you make a request, we have one month to respond to you. Please contact us at support@pdfbox.online.

6. CCPA / CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California consumers have the right to:

  • Request that a business that collects personal consumer data disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request that a business delete any personal data about the consumer that a business collected.
  • Right to opt-out of sale or sharing: PDF BOX does not sell or rent personal information or uploaded file data to third parties.

7. Children's Information (COPPA)

PDF BOX does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you think that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will do our best efforts to promptly remove such information from our records.

8. Security Measures

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols, including Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3/SSL encryption) for all data in transit, strict memory allocation limits, and automated file destruction scripts to prevent unauthorized data access.
