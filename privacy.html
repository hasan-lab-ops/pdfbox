Privacy Policy

File Processing

PDF BOX processes documents in your browser. Files selected for a tool are not uploaded to, stored by, or read by PDF BOX servers. Closing or refreshing the page clears files held in browser memory.

Information We Collect

PDF BOX does not require an account. We do not collect personal information when you use our tools. If you contact us via email, we receive the information you choose to include so that we can respond. We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Essential cookies: Remember your cookie preferences and consent choices
  • Advertising cookies: Display relevant advertisements through Google AdSense
  • Analytics cookies: Understand how visitors use our site (if enabled)

You can control cookies through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. For more information about how Google uses cookies for advertising, see Google's Cookie Policy.

Google AdSense and Advertising

PDF BOX uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. This is known as personalized advertising.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance

Even if you opt out of personalized ads, you may still see advertisements based on the content of this site or your general location.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: For displaying advertisements. See Google's Privacy Policy
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): To deliver open-source document-processing libraries and fonts. These providers may receive technical data like IP addresses and browser details. Your documents are not sent to these providers.

Your Rights (GDPR, CCPA, and Other Privacy Laws)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request information about data collected about you
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt-out of Sale: Under CCPA, you can opt out of the "sale" of personal information (note: we do not sell personal information)
  • Non-discrimination: You will not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us at hasan.saad898@gmail.com.

Data Retention

Since we do not store your files on our servers, there is no file data retention. Cookie data is retained according to your consent preferences and browser settings. Advertising partners may retain data according to their own policies.

Children's Privacy

PDF BOX is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Security

Since files are processed locally in your browser and not transmitted to our servers, your documents remain under your control. We recommend using secure, up-to-date browsers when using our tools.

Changes to This Policy
