Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At PDF BOX, we prioritize your privacy. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use our core tools.

2. File Processing

Most of our tools run entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. This means your files never leave your device.

For tools requiring advanced processing (such as PDF to Word, Excel, or PowerPoint conversions), your files are securely uploaded to our temporary processing servers. These files are encrypted in transit, processed in memory, and immediately deleted upon completion. We do not store, view, or retain your files.

3. Third-Party Advertising

We use third-party advertising companies, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Ads Settings.
